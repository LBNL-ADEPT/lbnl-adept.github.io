ADEPT

Advanced Data Engineering and Platform Technologies, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory

ADEPT is a Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory program building the Energy Sciences Area's first shared infrastructure for turning experimental data into AI-ready assets. A dedicated engineering team owns the connective tissue between bench instruments, a shared data lakehouse, and the knowledge systems that make scientific data reusable and machine-actionable.

What we build

  • Instrument ingestion pipelines. Standardized connectors from lab instruments to a Bronze/Silver/Gold medallion data lakehouse.
  • Data curation infrastructure. Sample identity, calibration versioning, and provenance tracking from raw signal to analysis-ready dataset.
  • Knowledge layer integration. Structured pathways from curated experimental data into AI and materials informatics systems.

How we work

The program is organized in three planes: instrument, platform, and knowledge. We are a co-development partner to ScienceIT, HPDF, Crucible, the Materials Project, and FORUM-AI, and we contribute upstream rather than forking. Datasets promoted to the curated tier are designed to receive a DOI through OSTI, so DOE public-access requirements are met as part of using the platform.

Scope

ADEPT operates across the Energy Sciences Area at LBNL, supporting chemical sciences, materials science, and foundry-scale experimental programs. Phase 1 (July 2026 to September 2027) pilots the full pipeline on gas chromatography instrumentation before replicating across instrument classes.
